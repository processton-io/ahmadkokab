  If you’ve ever wondered how hackers can intercept calls, steal OTPs, or track
  locations, the answer lies in an old telecom vulnerability: **SS7 (Signaling
  System No. 7)**. This decades-old protocol, still in use by major telecom
  operators worldwide, has been a goldmine for cybercriminals, private
  investigators, and even state surveillance programs. In Pakistan, where mobile
  banking and digital transactions are on the rise, the risks are even higher.
  This blog explores the **real dangers of SS7, its impact on privacy, and what
  authorities, telecom operators, and banks need to do about it.**
